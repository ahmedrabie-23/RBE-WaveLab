# Roadmap

This public documentation/community repository accompanies the published RBE WaveLab V1.0.0 desktop release. No dated product schedule or unapproved feature commitment is announced.

## Current approved directions

- Prepare clear English documentation and a community space for transmission-line learning.
- Record the owner-accepted skipped final Stable installation and Q05/Q06 uninstall/reinstallation as NOT TESTED — Owner-Accepted Risk; preserve historical RC3 Q02 FAIL and separate remediation PASS. Do not describe lifecycle qualification as complete.
- Preserve the published V1.0.0 release and disclose its bounded Windows 11 x64 qualification; no skipped test is promoted to PASS.
- Prepare the official website locally; public website deployment remains a separate authorization and has not occurred.

Suggestions can be discussed, but are not approved implementation work. Windows 10 qualification, other operating systems, transient simulation, hardware integration and additional major capabilities are not promised. Future versions and monetization beyond V1.x remain undecided. All V1.x releases and updates remain free under the [approved license](LICENSE.txt). No automatic updater or automatic binary-release workflow is enabled by this repository.
