## Choose the right starting mode

The five built-in recipes cover Wave Propagation, Reflection Coefficient, Standing Waves & VSWR, Lossy Transmission Lines and Distortionless Transmission Lines. Preconfigured Setup supplies a connected example. Equipment Ready supplies equipment for assembly. Empty Setup begins without equipment. These modes serve different learning objectives and should not be described as the same starting circuit.

## Read the guide as part of the experiment

Inspect objectives, supported settings and questions before Run. Predict what a change should do, use the instruments/plots to inspect a valid Current result, then compare your explanation. A copied expected number is not a substitute for checking the assumptions and physical units.

## Author a template

Experiment Builder organizes a supported setup and its educational content. Save/export `.wavelab-exp` for a portable template; `.wavelab` is the project format. Keep any test template in a permitted user-writable location and preserve original copies while checking portability. Do not rely on undisclosed absolute file paths.

## Respect visibility policy

Legitimate instrument measurements and protected theoretical variables are distinct. An instructor's policy may require deliberate **Reveal Results** for permitted protected values. **Hidden Throughout Exercise** must remain hidden even after reveal. Properties, instruments, existing plots, aliases, comparisons, exports and late asynchronous results must respect the same authority.

Changing a view or comparing another workbench must not bypass that policy or secretly solve a Manual source. If a quantity is hidden, do not interpret the absence as a physical zero or try to reconstruct a prohibited answer through an alias.

## Reuse responsibly

Use original or authorized educational material and nonconfidential configurations. Public community sharing must not expose private student information or proprietary engineering data. The application source remains proprietary even though authorized V1.x use is free. See [projects](/learn/guides/projects/) and [community](/community/) for the appropriate workflows.
