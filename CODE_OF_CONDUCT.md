# Community code of conduct

RBE WaveLab is a learning community for students, educators and engineers worldwide. Be respectful, patient and clear, especially when someone is learning a new concept or working in a second language.

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Provide constructive, reproducible technical feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, copyright and independent third-party rights.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, impersonate others or publish another person's private information.
- Do not upload passwords, confidential projects or proprietary engineering data.

Maintainers may moderate, edit or remove inappropriate public content, close off-topic threads, and restrict participation when necessary. Moderation and support are best-effort; no response deadline is promised.

For a sensitive conduct concern, contact [Ahmed Rabie on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmedrabie23) with a brief request for a private conversation. Do not post private evidence or personal details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
